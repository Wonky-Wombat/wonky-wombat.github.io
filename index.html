Five reads, one scan

The moods Catspace looks for.

Relaxed

Soft ears, slow blinks, loose posture.

Happy

Bright eyes, forward ears, an easy face.

Curious

Wide eyes, alert ears, leaning in.

Fearful

Flattened ears, dilated pupils, tense.

Aggressive

Pinned ears, fixed stare, coiled body.
